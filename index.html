    Why DesertBooker

    A desert trip is not a city break.

    On a general travel site the desert is a filter, sitting between beachfront and city centre. But what decides a desert trip is not what decides a hotel: how far out the camp is, how you reach it, which months are worth going, and what there is to do once the sun drops.

    DesertBooker is built by a team with more than twenty years of experience across hospitality, travel, marketplaces and technology.

    • Stays and experiences, together

      The camp, the camel trek and the dinner are one trip, so we treat them as one.

    • The practical part

      Distance, access, season and what is included, before you book rather than on arrival.

    • Straight from the hosts

      Camps describe their own places, and we check the details that matter.

    What we pay attention to

    Four things that decide a trip.

    Get these right and the trip is extraordinary. Get them wrong and no amount of thread count helps.

    • Getting there

      Some camps sit off a paved road. Others need a 4x4 and a guide who meets you at the edge of the sand.

    • The month you go

      The same camp is a different trip in January and in July. We show when each one is at its best.

    • How dark it gets

      Far from city light the sky is the reason people come, and some camps are far darker than others.

    • What you do there

      Camel treks, dune drives, falconry, dinner cooked over a fire. Usually the point of the trip, not an extra.

    Both sides

    Useful to the people who travel, and to the people who host.

    • For travellers

      Know the camp before you book it.

      What the place is like, how remote it is, how you get there and what is included, so where you arrive is what you chose.

    • For camps and operators

      Reach guests who came for the desert.

      Your camp is seen by people already looking for a desert stay, and described the way you would describe it: the access, the season, the sky, what is included. Not a row in a hotel list.

    United Arab Emirates

    More desert than most people expect.

    The UAE holds the northern edge of Rub' al Khali, the largest sand sea on earth, where the dunes rise red above the Liwa oases. It also holds gravel plains and date farms around Al Ain, and camps close enough to Dubai and Abu Dhabi to reach before dinner. This is the desert we know best.
